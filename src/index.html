Why the vial history matters

Field guide

Look for sex combs on the first tarsal segment of the male forelegs. Mature males usually have a darker, shorter abdominal tip. Young males can be pale, so check more than body colour.

These first-pass models have expanded wings. Meconium and unexpanded wings are not yet represented.

Worked example

In a separate example, a pale fly has visible foreleg sex combs. That identifies a male, so he is excluded from a female collection. A fly identified as female still needs an appropriate collection history before she can be used as a virgin.
