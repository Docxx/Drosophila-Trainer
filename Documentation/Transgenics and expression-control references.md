# Transgenics and expression-control lessons

Four open modules extend the existing course. They assume general biology knowledge and follow GAL4/UAS, chromosome notation and the relevant recombination lessons. Each has explicit objectives, a glossary, a four-stage animated guide, a separate worked example, guided questions, independent questions and an evidence-diagnosis question.

Existing lesson step sequences and stored progress are unchanged. The new lessons have separate identifiers. These modules teach experimental reasoning with known examples; they do not add unsupported transgenic genotypes to the stock-building engine.

## Coverage

- **Create and establish a transgenic line:** donor and recipient DNA; P-element versus phiC31 integration and targeted repair; G0 founders; germline transmission; separate F1 families; marker screening; junction, sequence and functional validation; stock maintenance.
- **GAL80:** inherited transgenes versus their activity; repression of GAL4; subtracting overlapping expression domains; ordinary GAL80 versus GAL80ts; connection to the existing TARGET timing model.
- **GeneSwitch:** GAL4 DNA-binding, progesterone-receptor ligand-binding and p65 activation regions; RU486 induction; driver specificity; basal activity and timing; vehicle and genetic controls; why regulation of ordinary GAL4 cannot simply be assumed for a different activation region.
- **Recombination: choose the mechanism:** female meiotic exchange, mitotic FLP/FRT clones, same-molecule excision and inversion, phiC31 integration, and homology-directed repair. The existing mapping, clone and stock-building activities remain the practical extensions.

## Sources

These sources support the mechanisms and experimental distinctions. The lesson prose and SVG diagrams are original teaching material, not reproduced figures or protocols.

- [Bischof et al. (2007), An optimized transgenesis system for Drosophila using germ-line-specific phiC31 integrases](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17360644/): defined attP recipient sites and phiC31-mediated transgenesis.
- [Fish et al. (2007), Creating transgenic Drosophila by microinjecting the site-specific phiC31 integrase mRNA and a transgene-containing donor plasmid](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17947973/): donor/recipient integration and recovery of stable lines.
- [FlyBase, GAL80 experimental tool report](https://flybase.org/reports/FBto0000150): repression of GAL4 and restriction of driver patterns.
- [McGuire et al. (2003), Spatiotemporal rescue of memory dysfunction in Drosophila](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14657498/): TARGET control of when and where GAL4 acts.
- [Osterwalder et al. (2001), A conditional tissue-specific transgene expression system using inducible GAL4](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11675495/): the GeneSwitch system.
- [Roman et al. (2001), P{Switch}, a system for spatial and temporal control of gene expression](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC60100/): GeneSwitch protein domains and drug-dependent activation.
- [Nicholson et al. (2008), Spatial and temporal control using GeneSwitch GAL4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2206072/): distinct driver requirements and driver-dependent induction.
- [Li and Stavropoulos (2016), Evaluation of ligand-inducible expression systems](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5068954/): validation and treatment controls.
- [Gratz et al. (2014), Highly specific and efficient CRISPR/Cas9-catalyzed homology-directed repair in Drosophila](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24478335/): targeted donor-mediated editing.
- [Cook et al. (2010), Systematic coverage and subdivision of the X chromosome with nested, Y-linked duplications](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2998296/): orientation-dependent FLP/FRT rearrangements.

The project's Trainer Specification and Prokop reference guides remain the curriculum baseline. Model limitations stay in each lesson's existing About this model panel. Actual construct records and local validated methods determine experimental conditions; the conceptual lessons supply no injection recipe or universal induction schedule.
